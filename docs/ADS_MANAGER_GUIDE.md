# AstroPress Ads Manager / Ad Inserter Guide

Welcome to the AstroPress **Ads Manager / Ad Inserter**, designed following the clean, powerful workflow of WordPress Ad Inserter.

---

### 1. Where to Open Ads Manager
1. Log into the AstroPress Admin Panel (`/admin` or `/wpadmin`).
2. In the left-hand admin navigation sidebar, click on **Ads Manager / Ad Inserter**.

---

### 2. How to Add an Ad
1. Click the prominent **"+ Add New Ad"** button at the top of the Ads Manager screen.
2. Provide an **Ad Block Name** (e.g. *Header Leaderboard* or *In-Article Banner*).
3. Select the **Ad Provider / Type**:
   - **Google AdSense**
   - **Custom Image Banner**
   - **Trusted Embed / HTML**
   - **Demo Ad (Test Mode)**
4. Paste your **Ad Unit Code** or configure your custom banner image URL and destination link.

---

### 3. How to Select Multiple Display Locations
Unlike rigid single-location systems, AstroPress lets you target multiple page types simultaneously using checkboxes:
- **Homepage**
- **Single Posts / Articles**
- **Static Pages**
- **Categories / Archives / Tags**
- **Sidebar**
- **Global Footer**

Selecting a location reveals its specific placement positions underneath it. You can select multiple locations and multiple positions at once.

---

### 4. How to Set Insertion Intervals
For repeated insertions such as **Between Article Paragraphs** or **Between Post Cards (Feed)**:
- Select the placement checkbox.
- Choose your desired **Insertion Interval** from the dropdown (e.g., *Every 2 paragraphs*, *Every 3 items*).

---

### 5. Global ON / OFF & Demo Mode Controls
- **Global Ads Switch**: Located in the top header bar. When toggled **OFF**, all site advertisements and demo ads are instantly hidden across the entire site without deleting your saved ad configurations.
- **Demo Mode Switch**: When toggled **ACTIVE**, displays clearly labeled `"DEMO AD — NOT A REAL AD"` banners at your configured placement positions so you can test and preview layouts immediately without a real AdSense account.

---

### 6. Managing Existing Ads
In the saved ads list, you can:
- **Pause / Activate**: Temporarily disable or re-enable individual ad blocks.
- **Edit**: Modify code, locations, or placements.
- **Duplicate**: Clone an ad block with a new unique ID.
- **Delete**: Remove an ad block with confirmation.
